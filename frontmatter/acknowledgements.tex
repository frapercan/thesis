I am deeply grateful to my co-supervisors, David Orellana-Mart\'{i}n and
Ana M.\ Rojas, for their guidance and continued support throughout this
doctoral research. David Orellana-Mart\'{i}n provided close direction on
the computational and engineering dimensions of the work, offering
rigorous critical review at each stage of the system's development.
Ana M.\ Rojas brought essential expertise in protein function and
evolutionary biology, shaping the biological framing of the research
and guiding the path towards publication and defence. Their
complementary perspectives made this thesis possible, and I am grateful
to both of them for taking it seriously.

The biology group at the Centro Andaluz de Biolog\'{i}a del Desarrollo
(CABD) offered an environment in which computational and biological
thinking met productively. Conversations and interactions within
Ana M.\ Rojas's group were a constant source of domain knowledge that
informed PROTEA's design and evaluation.

PROTEA builds on the FANTASIA codebase, the predecessor system developed
within the same research context. I thank the authors of the FANTASIA
platform for the intellectual groundwork they laid: PROTEA owes its
initial direction to the questions FANTASIA raised and the limitations
it exposed. The architectural decisions taken in PROTEA, from the
plugin registry to the FAISS-based retrieval layer, were shaped in
direct response to that prior work.

The \#19 final team ranking in the Critical Assessment of protein
Function Annotation (CAFA~6) reflects a collective effort. I contributed
the technical infrastructure and prediction pipeline described in this
thesis, and I thank the CAFA~6 team collaborators for the shared work
that made that placement possible. PROTEA, as presented here, is the
productisation of that technical contribution and stands as an
independent open-source platform.

Finally, I thank my family and close friends for their patience and
steady encouragement throughout these years.
