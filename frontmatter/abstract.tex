\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Fewer than 0.02\,\% of the proteins deposited in UniProt carry experimentally
validated functional annotations. Computational methods attempt to close this gap by
transferring Gene Ontology (GO) knowledge from characterised proteins to unknown ones,
yet the ecosystem of tools for doing so is fragmented across notebooks and one-off
scripts, and most published benchmarks conflate training and test annotations through
temporal leakage, inflating reported performance.

This doctoral thesis presents \textbf{PROTEA} (\textit{PROtein funcTional Embedding-based
Annotation}), designed and maintained as author and sole maintainer by Francisco Miguel
P\'{e}rez Canales, an open-source distributed platform that automates the full pipeline
from raw protein sequence to structured GO predictions. PROTEA couples protein language
model (PLM) embeddings from eight backends (including ESM-2, ESM-C, ProstT5, ProtT5,
Ankh and ESM-3) with approximate $k$-nearest-neighbour search over 527,000 reviewed
UniProt sequences. A contracts-first plugin architecture spans seven satellite
repositories (\texttt{protea-contracts}, \texttt{protea-method}, \texttt{protea-sources},
\texttt{protea-runners}, \texttt{protea-backends}, \texttt{cafaeval-protea}, and
\texttt{protea-reranker-lab}) wired to the core via Python \texttt{entry\_points}, so
that new backends, annotation sources and ranking methods can be added without
modifying the core platform. The platform ships five deployment modes covering
Docker\,Compose development, Docker\,Compose bundle, Docker Swarm, Helm/Kubernetes, and
SLURM, and exposes OpenTelemetry-instrumented observability across FastAPI, SQLAlchemy
and pika.

Prediction quality is improved by a LightGBM re-ranker trained on a 56-feature schema
combining embedding distance, Needleman-Wunsch and Smith-Waterman alignment statistics,
NCBI-taxonomy-based distance metrics, and GO-graph ancestry embeddings (anc2vec).
Training follows a leakage-free temporal holdout over twelve consecutive GOA releases.
Under this protocol a $3^{3}$ hyperparameter grid search on the NK-BPO task yields
$F_{\max}$ in the range 0.4386--0.4669, and cafaeval re-validation of the 56-feature
re-ranker (three-seed replication, average lab $F_{\max}$ 0.2881) returns 0.5427,
exceeding the
historical hybrid-picker ceiling of 0.4400. Feature-ablation experiments identify
\texttt{anc2vec\_query} as the dominant contributor, with a leave-one-family-out mean
$\Delta F_{\max}$ of $+0.1449$. Bootstrap confidence intervals against the KNN baseline
show $p < 0.0001$ across all nine ablation cells, with per-cell deltas ranging from
$+0.03$ to $+0.43$. The binary-label per-category LightGBM champion, replicated across
three seeds on \texttt{bench-v1-K5-v226-lineage-prostt5}, achieves NK+LK combined
cafaeval $F_{\max} = \mathbf{0.7291 \pm 0.0028}$ (LB.3 paired bootstrap, 6/6 NK+LK
cells strictly positive at 95\,\% confidence), the primary publishable result for
NK and LK categories. These gains collapse almost entirely when temporal leakage is
introduced, underscoring the importance of rigorous evaluation methodology.

The experimental work was a component of a team ranking of \#19 in CAFA~6,
validating the approach in a blinded international benchmark; PROTEA is the
post-CAFA productisation, not the submission entrant. Three pre-built containers were
submitted to the LAFA public benchmark, demonstrating deployment across development,
cloud, HPC (Apptainer) and airgapped environments. On the LAFA continuously updated
public benchmark, measured once on the sealed September~2025 to March~2026 target frame
and scored with the benchmark's own harness, the offline research champion reaches
\textbf{first place} overall (IA-weighted micro-$F$ of 0.391, against 0.381 for the
previous leader), and the same method reproduced natively on the platform and served
live from the \texttt{/benchmark} surface reaches a productized mean of 0.3745. It
combines the homology backbone with a learned combiner that adds a direct full-catalogue
predictor and, for partly characterised proteins, the cross-aspect functions that
habitually accompany what is already known; every ingredient is chosen on an earlier
window and confirmed on a held-out split that the sealed measurement never sees.

The doctoral contribution is threefold: (1) PROTEA as a reproducible, operationally
auditable platform with a seven-repository plugin ecosystem and five deployment modes;
(2) a leakage-free reranker pipeline that quantifies the true lift of feature
engineering over PLM embeddings; (3) a first-place offline research result on the
LAFA continuous benchmark (0.391), with a natively reproduced, live-served
productized figure (0.3745) as an independently verifiable performance reference.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} protein function prediction, gene ontology, protein
